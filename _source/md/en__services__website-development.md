Design and Approval

+

We begin by gathering your input and aligning on goals, followed by designing the layout, user experience, and overall look of the site. After finalizing the design, we confirm the structure before moving to development.

Step 2

Template Development

+

With the design approved, we develop the website templates, including coding the homepage and key templates, ensuring mobile responsiveness and performance optimization.

Content Creation & Integration

+

In this stage, we integrate your content, applying SEO best practices to ensure everything is formatted, aligned with your branding, and optimized for search visibility.

Step 4

Final QA, Launch & Post Migration

+

After completing final quality checks and bug fixes, we launch the site and monitor performance. Post-launch, we ensure smooth migration and offer support for any issues that may arise.

## FAQ

Are these websites fully custom-built?

Yes, we build custom websites tailored to your specific needs and business goals. From design to functionality, everything is developed to ensure a unique and high-performing website that aligns with your brand.

How long does it take to launch a website?

The typical development timeline is about 2 months from design approval to launch. More complex projects, like e-commerce or SaaS sites, may require additional time for development and integration.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

We work with a wide range of businesses, from startups to established companies, across industries such as SaaS, e-commerce, B2B, and corporate sectors. No matter your business type, we can create a custom solution that works for you.

Can I request revisions or changes?

Yes, revisions and changes can be requested during the development process. We ensure your website meets your expectations and offer post-launch support for any further adjustments or updates.
