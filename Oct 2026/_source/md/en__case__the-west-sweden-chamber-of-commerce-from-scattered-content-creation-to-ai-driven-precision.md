# The West Sweden Chamber of Commerce – From scattered content creation to AI-driven precision

CASE STUDY \| Generative AI

The West Sweden Chamber of Commerce needed a smarter path from idea to finished content. Internally, too much time was spent rewriting the same types of material, while demands for content production continued to rise. With GO MO Group, they built a custom GPT that now guides the entire process, from brief to publication-ready draft.

“It was hard to stay consistent. We want one voice – no matter who’s writing.”

### That’s where we started – with the right questions, not the right answers

GO MO’s team worked closely with the communications lead and the AI pilot group. We mapped how they worked, what took the most time and what could be automated without losing heart or credibility.

We built a private GPT with embedded instructions, tailored to the Chamber’s tone of voice, content types and structural needs.

![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201541%20958'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### How we solved it

**Interviews and mapping.**

We mapped the full writing process and defined the GPT’s role.

**Data gathering.**

We uploaded tone guides, terminology lists and high-quality sample texts.

**Prompt design.**

The model was trained with clear rules for structure, citations and voice.

**Iterative testing.**

The marketing and policy teams gave feedback – we fine-tuned until the GPT delivered right on the first try.

![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202560%201247'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### The impact? Clear and measurable

All four companies now have:

- Time from idea to publication-ready text cut in half
- Consistent tone, even when multiple people write
- The team became self-sufficient, less need for external writers and “text editors”
- AI is used as support, not replacement, lowering the threshold for more people to write

At the same time, they got free visibility. The GPT solution was featured in courses and on the Chamber’s website. New participants reached out, not to learn GPT, but to get the same tool.

“We save time, we improve quality – and we sound like ourselves. That’s exactly what we wanted.”

![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201030%20429'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
