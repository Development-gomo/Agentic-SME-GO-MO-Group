# AI in marketing: the end of website traffic as we know it

You've adapted your sales and marketing strategy to the evolving B2B commerce landscape, fully embracing the increasing digitalization of your customers' purchasing behavior - great! Even better, you've started to generate a significant number of relevant leads organically. In fact, organic traffic has become one of your largest sources of leads and revenue. **But what if that organic traffic were to decrease by 50%? How would this impact top line revenue?**

![](https://www.gomogroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/a_photograph_photorealistic_human_eye_closeup_with.webp)

According to Gartner's latest report on the Future of Search, **organic traffic is expected to decrease up to 50% by 2028** with the rise of Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and the increasing prominence of paid ads in the search market.

At GO MO Group, we've anticipated the shift in digital sales processes due to generative AI for a while. We have in our research and predictions clearly found indications that digital brands will experience the impact of GEO even sooner than expected. Many companies will see **organic search traffic drop** **by up to 25%** or more within two years time as consumers increasingly turn to generative AI-powered search for their queries. Data from both Gartner and SearchEngineLand have later supported this prediction. Preparing for this shift is critical to maintain visibility and relevance in the digital landscape.

## What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is a strategy focused on optimizing content for AI-powered search engines like Google's Search Generative Experience (SGE). Traditionally, SERPs (search engine result page) have been organized with ads at the top, followed by snippets, top organic results, and additional ads. However, with GEO, a large generative AI box will appear at the top of the page, providing AI-generated answers before anything else. This disrupts the current order by pushing traditional results further down, making it essential for brands to optimize their content for AI-generated responses.

[![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202048%20646'%3E%3C/svg%3E)](https://www.gomogroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Screenshot-Image-1.png.webp)

## What GEO means for your business

As businesses become increasingly reliant on digital channels for lead generation, the search landscape is undergoing a significant transformation. GEO will revolutionize how search engines deliver results, leveraging advanced AI to provide users with more comprehensive, context-rich answers directly within the search engine results pages (SERPs). This shift is already a reality in markets where GEO has been rolled out, with Google AI often taking the top spots, even above paid ads, in over **92% of all searches** according to Gartner.

### Disruption in traditional digital strategies

For businesses that have built lead generation strategies around organic search, this trend poses a considerable challenge. As users find answers directly within the SERPs, fewer are clicking through to websites, leading to a predicted decrease in organic traffic. Simultaneously, **the cost of paid search advertising is estimated to increase by 17% on YoY basis**. The rise of GEO represents not just a challenge, but also a significant opportunity for those willing to evolve. By implementing a GEO strategy and diversifying your traffic sources, you’ll enhance your digital presence and position your business to emerge as a winner in this technological shift.

## How to build a strategy optimized for GEO

From where we stand, two clear paths emerge for optimizing your marketing strategy with GEO. These paths are complementary, and we recommend adopting both. The first focuses on **re-building fundament by preparing website and digital presence** for the rise of GEO. The second involves increasing flexibility by exploring alternative digital strategies to **diversify your organic traffic sources**. Below, we'll outline key activities to implement and highlight two potential traffic sources you can leverage to ensure a balanced and future-proof strategy.

### Activities you should focus on today

#### Implement schemas

Structured data helps search engines better understand the content on your site, increasing the chances of your pages being featured in AI-generated search results. One of the first steps in adapting to GEO is to enhance your website's structure by implementing schemas. Implementing schemas allows you to communicate directly with the search engine, providing it with context about content, which is crucial as GEO relies heavily on understanding the nuances of user queries.

#### Rebuild FAQ content strategy that **_actually_** caters to the user

Users increasingly expect search engines to provide immediate, relevant answers to their questions. By developing a robust FAQ-strategy that genuinely addresses common queries, you can align your content with the needs of your audience. This approach has been successfully implemented by digitally mature companies, which uses FAQs and other content to directly answer search queries, increasing their visibility within GEO-driven search results. We suggest becoming even more client-centric by addressing the questions your clients ask regularly. Involve your sales or client success teams to identify common sales objections or frequently asked questions **by clients**. By addressing these concerns through your content, you can streamline the buying experience and ease potential client hesitation.

#### Expand and improve presence on review platforms

As GEO increasingly prioritizes content that conveys trust and authority, your brand's reputation on review platforms becomes even more critical. While trust and authority have always been important under today's SEO E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) guidelines, GEO will elevate their significance even further. It's crucial to incentivize satisfied clients to leave positive reviews and actively engage on these platforms. A strong presence on review sites can significantly influence how your brand is perceived by both consumers and search engines, enhancing your chances of being highlighted in GEO results. This is also a great way to diversify your traffic sources as many use review platforms to take well informed purchase decisions.

#### Develop a content strategy focused on branded, multimodal and **unique** content

High-quality content has always been important, but in the era of GEO, it's more crucial than ever. Gartner reports that **over 64% of all results in Google SGE links to publishers' sites**. Meanwhile only 17% link to branded sites today.

![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202048%201400'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

Focus on creating branded content that is not only informative but also unique and multimodal. This means incorporating text, images, video, and even interactive elements to engage your audience across multiple touchpoints. Curating internal documents and producing high-quality branded content that positions them as thought leaders and drives meaningful traffic. Many companies possess unique data pointers and documentation that could support a lot of insightful content pieces - it's time to leverage these data pointers to create meaningful communication and **add value to your target audience**.

### Two examples of how to diversify your traffic sources

#### Leverage video and YouTube

Video content will remain a powerful tool for capturing attention and conveying your brand's message in an engaging way. With platforms like YouTube becoming increasingly influential, it's essential to leverage video to connect with your target audience. Create content that answers natural language queries, offers insights, or showcases your products in action. By doing so, you can establish your **brand as a trusted resource in your industry**, driving traffic and building a loyal following. As mentioned above, multimodal content will be one of the cornerstones of a successful GEO strategy. Video will be a way of supporting your content stream and a unique way for your target audience to interact with your brand and offerings.

#### Prospecting and outreach

Take a more proactive approach to client acquisition. With a thriving economy and strong inbound lead generation, many brands have become "fat and happy," relying on easy growth. However, as demand declines and competition intensifies, it's time to adopt a more aggressive strategy. **Align your efforts with your ideal customer profiles (ICPs)** and engage in targeted prospecting and personalized outreach. This approach not only ensures relevance and product fit but also reduces dependence on external factors, making your business more resilient.

## To act now, or react later

The change is inevitable - it's only a matter of time. As the head of your company's marketing or sales department, you soon need to decide on how to approach this change. Have in mind that this is not only a risk, but also a huge opportunity. At GO MO Group, we've spent the past year preparing our clients, equipping them with the knowledge and strategies needed to emerge victorious from this technological evolution.

With that said - we are still humble in that this has never really been done and nobody really knows **_exactly_** what to do. Though we can be sure of one thing - something needs to be done and not having a strategy _is not a strategy._

1\. How will generative AI impact website traffic for B2B companies?

Generative AI is expected to reduce organic website traffic by up to 50% by 2028, as AI-powered search engines like Google SGE provide direct answers in search results—limiting user clicks to websites. Hopefully, this will not reduce number of conversions, but much is still uncertain.

2\. What is GEO and how can it help maintain search visibility?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the strategy of optimizing your content, website and general digital footprint to appear in AI-generated answers. It focuses on structured data, trusted reviews, and multimodal content to maintain visibility as traditional SEO loses impact.

3\. What should B2B marketers do to prepare for GEO-driven search disruption?

To adapt, businesses should implement schema markup, build strong FAQ content, engage on review platforms, and create branded, multimodal content. This helps align with how AI curates search results and enhances discoverability.
