# ChatGPT Ads vs Google Ads: what we’ve learned so far

_Last updated: 15 October 2026. We update the data as the pilots continue - more to come._

![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201920%201080'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

In August, ChatGPT Ads opened to advertisers in 31 European markets, Sweden and Germany among them. For B2B marketing leaders the question came quickly: is this a new search channel, and does it deserve a budget of its own?

Since then we have run ChatGPT Ads alongside Google Ads for several B2B enterprise clients, with the same topics and the same landing pages on both channels. The pilots are still in an early phase and the samples small, so read the numbers as directional. **Seven lessons stand out.**

Lesson 01

## 1\. You can see far less of what ChatGPT is doing

In one pilot, a short keyword list matched thousands of distinct search terms, most of them through broad match and many clearly off-target. Only a fraction of the keywords registered any impressions at all.

ChatGPT Ads currently offers no query or conversation report, no match-type breakdown and no exclusion controls. You cannot see which conversations your ad appeared in, or remove the poor ones. For now, the levers are the context you target and the page people land on.

Lesson 02

## 2\. ChatGPT reaches more people, earlier in the research

Across our two pilots, we ran ChatGPT Ads and Google Ads on the same topics from mid-September to early October. The figures below combine both pilots and are rounded.

| Metric (both pilots, rounded) | ChatGPT Ads | Google Ads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impressions | ~500,000 | ~130,000 |
| Clicks | ~4,100 | ~8,400 |
| Click-through rate | ~0.8% | ~6.5% |
| Average CPC | ~SEK 20 | ~SEK 18 |

Combined, ChatGPT delivered about four times Google's impressions. Google's click-through rate was about eight times higher, and it delivered about twice as many clicks. Cost per click was close: about SEK 20 on ChatGPT and about SEK 18 on Google. Budgets and bidding setups differed between the channels (more on that in lesson 7), so read the gaps as directional.

The theme-level results explain the gap. ChatGPT performed best on broad research questions: comparing technologies, weighing options, working out which suppliers do what. Those are the conversations a buyer has before they know which supplier to search for. Google was strongest on transactional searches, where people already knew what they were looking for.

> Our reading: ChatGPT Ads brings you into the research conversation earlier, with a wider and less qualified audience. That makes it a complement to search, and worth judging on those terms.

Lesson 03

## 3\. Early traffic quality looks promising

Reach only matters if the visitors are the right ones. In another pilot we compared ChatGPT Ads visitors with Google paid visitors who landed on the same commercial pages.

ChatGPT visitors had an engagement rate of about 86% against about 83% for Google, and viewed about 10.5 pages per session against about 10. Average engagement time per session was shorter. The sample is still under 20 ChatGPT sessions, and neither channel has produced a conversion yet. Even so, these visitors browse like commercial traffic, which is reason enough for a longer test.

Lesson 04

## 4\. Test the platform guidance

![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201000%20717'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

ChatGPT Ads uses context hints to describe the conversations where an ad may be relevant. We tested the same themes in two formats: lists of keywords and topics, and full conversational statements. OpenAI's guidance recommends clear, descriptive phrases over short keywords. In our test, the keyword and topic hints led on click-through rate, CPC, impressions and clicks.

It is one test, and a good reason to check platform advice against your own audience before following it.

Lesson 05

## 5\. Design for mobile first

In both pilots, more than 80% of ChatGPT Ads impressions were on mobile, close to 90% combined. On Google Ads, the mobile share was about 63% for the same advertisers and periods. If ChatGPT is part of the plan, the landing page has to work on a phone first.

Lesson 06

## 6\. Measurement breaks easily

In one pilot, the tracking URLs used a "?" where an "&" belonged. GA4 read the whole string as one source, most ad sessions fell into "Unassigned", and a standard paid-traffic filter found only about one in seven sessions. We caught and fixed it, but it shows how easy it is to misjudge a new channel on broken data.

Before`…?utm_source=chatgpt_ad?utm_medium=paid?utm_campaign=…`

After`…?utm_source=chatgpt_ad&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=…`

Before launch, use one UTM structure across both channels, verify a live click in GA4 Realtime and decide which key events count as conversions. Then give the test time. We are holding one pilot for at least 30 days, aiming for 300-400 sessions, before judging conversion.

Lesson 07

## 7\. Compare like with like

Setup differences can easily be mistaken for channel differences. In one pilot, ChatGPT ran on manual CPC while Google used Maximize Clicks, which affects cost and delivery on its own. Compare matched pages and matched date ranges, and benchmark ChatGPT only against the Google campaigns that target the same audience.

## Our summary so far

ChatGPT Ads reach more people earlier in the research phase, at a cost per click close to Google's, with much less visibility and control. Google Ads remains the stronger channel for transactional searches, and the more transparent one to manage. For most B2B companies, that makes ChatGPT a channel to test alongside search, with the measurement in place to judge it. **Conversion is the open question, and the next stage of our pilots is set up to answer it.**

## Frequently asked questions

ChatGPT Ads vs Google Ads: which is better, and should B2B companies use both?

Each channel does a different job, so most B2B companies benefit from both. In our pilots, ChatGPT Ads reached buyers earlier, during broad research, with about four times Google's impressions at a similar cost per click. Google Ads delivered a click-through rate about eight times higher on specific, transactional searches. We recommend giving ChatGPT Ads its own test budget alongside search.

Do ChatGPT Ads work for B2B companies?

Early signs are positive for reaching buyers in the research phase. In one pilot, ChatGPT Ads visitors engaged at the same level as Google's commercial search traffic, on a small sample. One constraint matters for B2B: ads show only to users on the Free and Go plans, so buyers on Business and Enterprise accounts are out of reach. Treat it as an early-funnel channel for now.

Should we test ChatGPT Ads now, or wait?

Testing now means you learn the channel while it is new and build your own benchmarks before competitors do. Across our pilots, cost per click came in close to Google's on the same topics. Keep the test structured: a set budget, clean tracking, a like-for-like Google comparison and an agreed conversion definition, run for at least 30 days.

How much does it cost to advertise on ChatGPT?

ChatGPT Ads charges per impression (CPM) for reach campaigns and per click (CPC) for click campaigns. OpenAI recommends bids of USD 3-5 per click. Across our two pilots, the average cost per click came to about SEK 20. On Google Ads, it was about SEK 18 for the same topics.

What results are advertisers seeing from ChatGPT Ads?

Across two pilots, ChatGPT Ads delivered about 500,000 impressions and 4,100 clicks, with a click-through rate of about 0.8% and a cost per click of about SEK 20. On the same topics, Google Ads had a click-through rate of about 6.5% and a cost per click of about SEK 18. Engagement on the landing page matched Google's commercial traffic. Conversion data is still coming in, and we will update this article as it does.

Which are the best ChatGPT Ads agencies in Sweden and Europe?

Look for an agency that has run live ChatGPT Ads campaigns alongside Google Ads, sets up tracking before launch and compares the two channels like for like. GO MO Group runs ChatGPT Ads pilots for B2B enterprise clients across several European markets, using the approach described in this article.

Do ChatGPT ads affect ChatGPT's answers?

According to OpenAI, ads run on systems separate from the chat model, and answers stay independent of advertisers. Paid ads and visibility in ChatGPT's answers are two separate levers. Showing up in the answers themselves takes generative engine optimization (GEO) work on your own content.
