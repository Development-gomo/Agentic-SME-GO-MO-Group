# Brand systems   and identity

Brand systems built through strategy, clarity, and thoughtful design. Each identity is created to stay consistent across touchpoints while allowing the brand to express itself with confidence and purpose.

## Innosearch

Innosearch’s brand identity was designed to feel approachable, confident, and optimistic.

Cheerful by design

The visual language balances clarity with warmth through bold color, friendly typography, and simple graphic elements. Designed for a modern SEO agency, the identity communicates expertise without feeling corporate and scales seamlessly across presentations, sales material, and everyday communication.

## Novoterm

Novoterm’s brand identity was designed to convey clarity and trust. Built around the letter N as a strong visual anchor, the system uses a restrained blue palette and modern typography to communicate professionalism while remaining contemporary across brand presentations and communication material.

## Panea

Panea’s brand system was developed to ensure consistency across multiple applications. Working from an existing logo, we created a brand manual and designed key assets including corporate decks, print collateral, stationery, and digital communication. A custom line art element was created to use as a unifying visual thread across the brand.
