# How innovation hub HighFive turned AI potential into real business value

CASE STUDY \| Generative AI

### From exploration to execution. This is how five companies made AI actionable

HighFive, an innovation hub dedicated to strengthening Halland’s business community, wanted to explore how generative AI could create real value beyond the buzz. To do this, they launched an initiative with Range Servant, Lagafors, NMC, Ergosafe – four sales- and marketing–focused companies, all facing the same question

### “How can we use AI as a tool, not just an experiment?”

Without a shared model and structure, the risk was that each company would act in isolation, lose focus, and stall before achieving impact. Through shared workshops and targeted coaching from GO MO Group, CEOs and commercial teams gained tools to identify obstacles, set clear goals and move forward with confidence

_**The result: clearer direction, sharper priorities and concrete strategies for using AI to increase revenue or reduce cost.**_

### What we did

We began with two shared sessions that brought together leaders and teams from all four companies. The goal was to build a common understanding of how AI could support either **commercial growth or operational efficiency**.

We used a **straightforward framework**: where are you now, where do you want to be, and what is standing in the way. Participants learned how to prompt, how to apply AI to existing processes and what is required to create real outcomes.

In the next phase, we met with each company individually. We identified bottlenecks and presented tailored proposals that matched their **business priorities**.

![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%202048%201152'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

### What changed

All four companies now have:

- A clear view of how AI can **support sales or operations**
- A shared approach to move from insight to action
- **Greater confidence** in how to use the technology
- **A defined next step** shaped to their specific situation

“This was not a training session. It was a starting point. Now we know what to focus on and how to get there.”

## What other companies can take from this

Many companies talk about AI but lack the structure to turn ideas into action. HighFive shows what happens when leadership and teams align, when learning is grounded in business reality and when technology becomes a tool for actual progress.

_It was about moving forward. Together._
