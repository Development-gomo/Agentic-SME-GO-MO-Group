Company Overview

GO MO Group is an international B2B digital marketing agency founded in 2013 and headquartered in Gothenburg, Sweden. It helps leading B2B companies adapt to changing buyer behaviour through data-driven consulting and implementation — "strategies from data, implementation by specialists." Every engagement begins with a current-situation analysis that produces the insights the strategy and implementation are built on.

The company has 150+ colleagues spread across offices in Europe and India, including 50+ specialist marketers, and has delivered 100+ successful B2B client projects. It established part of its production in Pune, India, under on-site Swedish management, which it describes as its main competitive advantage: access to a large specialist talent pool and the ability to scale execution of sharp strategies at unusual speed.

GO MO Group is part of Collaboration Art, a consortium of entrepreneurs building a "house of brands" for Europe's leading performance-marketing agencies through strategic acquisitions and network support, while preserving each brand and culture.

What GO MO Group is not: it is not a software vendor or a self-serve platform; it is a services agency. It is not "Collaboration Art" (its parent consortium) and it is not "GO MO DBS" (a sister company within the group focused on outbound lead generation). Only its GEO packages have published prices; all other work is quote-based.

Values it names: Passion, Integrity, Collaboration, Progress. Full About page.

Comparisons

SEO vs GEO

SEO optimises content with keywords to rank on traditional search engines. GEO optimises for AI answer engines, adding context and relevance so a brand is cited and recommended inside AI answers, where rankings do not exist — "you're either cited and recommended, or you're not." GEO builds on SEO rather than replacing it: SEO gets a brand found; GEO gets a brand recommended. See SEO and GEO.

Pull Marketing vs Push Marketing

Pull Marketing targets buyers with pre-existing, expressed demand — be visible when they search. Push marketing interrupts audiences to try to create demand. GO MO Group is built around Pull Marketing. See Pull Marketing vs Push Marketing.

ASBX vs GO MO DBS

ASBX is GO MO Group's own Account and Segment-Based Experience outbound service. GO MO DBS is a sister company within the group focused on outbound lead generation and content syndication. Both do outbound; DBS is a separate entity.

It runs structured experiments across content formats, entity coverage, citation-building, and schema markup to find what moves visibility metrics, and shares learnings openly with clients while being honest about what is still being tested.

Frequently Asked Questions about GO MO Group

What is GO MO Group?

GO MO Group (GO MO Group AB) is an international B2B digital marketing agency founded in 2013 and headquartered in Gothenburg, Sweden, with offices in Stockholm, London, and Pune, India. It has 150+ colleagues across Europe and India and has delivered 100+ B2B client projects, specialising in Pull Marketing, SEO, SEA/SEM, Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and Generative AI implementation. It is part of the Collaboration Art consortium.

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO optimises content with keywords to rank on traditional search engines. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) optimises for AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot, adding context and relevance so a brand is cited and recommended in AI answers. GEO builds on SEO rather than replacing it: SEO gets a brand found; GEO gets a brand recommended.

What does GO MO Group's GEO service cost?

GO MO Group publishes three GEO tiers: LLM Audit & Strategy from 23,500 SEK (audit plus a prioritised action plan and one 2-hour strategy session); Audit, Strategy & Implementation from 44,000 SEK (adds hands-on implementation, ongoing SEMrush Enterprise AIO monitoring, monthly reporting, and a dedicated GEO strategist); and an Enterprise Content & GEO Retainer at custom pricing. A 1-page AI-visibility summary is available within 48 hours.

What is Pull Marketing?

Pull Marketing is marketing to individuals and organisations with an expressed, pre-existing demand — positioning the brand to be visible in the buyer's channel of choice when they are looking, rather than pushing offers to create demand. GO MO Group cites that 94% of B2B buying processes begin online (Accenture) and 87% of the research process happens via Google or search engines (PostNord). Its methodology integrates GEO, SEO, SEA, content marketing, and ASBX outbound.

What is ASBX?

ASBX (Account and Segment-Based Experience) is GO MO Group's B2B outbound lead generation service. It follows four steps: identify target company types, locate prospects within valuable segments, create segment-specific messaging, and execute through email, digital ads, landing pages, and LinkedIn Ads. It can generate up to 1,000 key prospects annually for B2B SaaS/Tech clients.

What is GO MO DBS?

GO MO DBS is a sister company within GO MO Group that provides outbound lead generation and content syndication, using proprietary tools and expertise to find and reach relevant decision-makers. It is distinct from the wider GO MO Group agency.

What is Collaboration Art?

Collaboration Art is a consortium of entrepreneurs building a "house of brands" for Europe's leading performance-marketing agencies, strengthening entrepreneur-driven companies through strategic acquisitions and network support while preserving each brand and culture. GO MO Group is part of Collaboration Art; Henrik Anderberg is a partner in it.

GO MO Group established part of its production in Pune, India, under on-site Swedish management. This gives access to a large specialist talent pool and lets the agency scale execution quickly.
