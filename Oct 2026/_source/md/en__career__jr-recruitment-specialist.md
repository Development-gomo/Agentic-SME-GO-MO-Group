# Jr. Recruitment Specialist

[Apply for this job](https://www.gomogroup.com/en/career-form/?job=jr-recruitment-specialist)

## Scope of work

**Sourcing & Pipeline Support:**

- Source candidates using job portals, LinkedIn, social platforms, and internal databases
- Build and maintain a steady pipeline of relevant candidates
- Support in experimenting with new sourcing strategies

**Screening & Candidate Interaction:**

- Conduct initial screening calls to assess basic fit (skills, communication, intent)
- Maintain detailed candidate notes and evaluations
- Ensure a positive and professional candidate experience

**Interview Coordination:**

- Schedule interviews and coordinate between candidates and hiring managers
- Track interview progress and follow up for feedback
- Ensure smooth and timely communication across all stakeholders

**Recruitment Operations & HR Support:**

- Maintain trackers, reports, and candidate databases
- Support documentation and offer process coordination
- Assist in onboarding activities and HR coordination tasks, External vendor coordination and Employee Engagement

## Key qualification criteria

- 0–1 year of hands-on experience in recruitment or talent acquisition
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
- Basic understanding of recruitment lifecycle and hiring processes
- Familiarity with sourcing platforms like LinkedIn, Naukri,Wellfound,Cutshort  etc.
- Strong organizational and multitasking abilities
- Residing in Pune or willing to relocate
- Bachelor’s degree in any field

## Preferred Attributes

- Exposure to hiring for Digital Marketing or Tech roles
- Ability to work in a fast-paced, target-driven environment
- Strong learning mindset with attention to detail
- Basic knowledge of recruitment tools and ATS systems
- Awareness or curiosity about using AI tools in recruitment
- High ownership, responsiveness, and “get things done” attitude
