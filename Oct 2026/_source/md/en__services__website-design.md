## Our creative process,   Supercharged by AI

Our streamlined process combines AI efficiency with design expertise to craft visuals that elevate your brand and meet your marketing goals.

[Get started](https://www.gomogroup.com/en/services/website-design-new/#service-page-form-section)

Step 1

Briefing & Alignment

+

We begin with a focused intake to understand your goals, audience, brand guidelines, and creative requirements. This sets clear expectations and defines scope, timelines, and success criteria.

Step 2

AI‑Powered Exploration

+

Using Generative AI tools, we rapidly explore concepts, layouts, visuals, and directions. This phase allows us to test multiple ideas quickly, accelerate ideation, and identify the strongest creative routes.

Step 3

Design Refinement & Strategy

+

Our designers refine the selected concepts with human-led UX thinking, visual hierarchy, messaging, and conversion strategy. AI supports iteration speed, while designers ensure quality, clarity, and brand consistency.

Step 4

Delivery & Feedback Loop

+

We deliver polished, production-ready assets and gather your feedback. Revisions are handled efficiently through our AI-assisted workflow, ensuring fast turnarounds and a final output aligned with your vision.

## FAQ

Are these designs fully AI-generated?

Not entirely. We use Generative AI to speed up and scale the creative process, but visual is reviewed, refined, and finalized by our experts to ensure quality and brand alignment.

How does AI improve the design process?

AI helps us explore more concepts faster, automate repetitive tasks, and speed up production. This means quicker turnarounds, more creative options, and efficient scaling—while human designers focus on strategy, UX, and storytelling.

Will the designs match our brand guidelines?

Yes. Brand alignment is central to our process. We train prompts, workflows, and design decisions around your brand guidelines, and our designers ensure every output feels cohesive, intentional, and on-brand.

Can I request revisions or changes?

Yes, absolutely. We include revision rounds as part of our process to ensure the final output meets your expectations. Because our workflow is AI-assisted, iterations are faster and more efficient—while our designers ensure every change maintains quality, clarity, and brand consistency.
