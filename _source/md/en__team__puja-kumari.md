# Meet Puja

I am GO MO’s User Experience Manager. I lead the Design and Development departments from the office in Pune, India. At heart, I have always been a problem solver who believes that great customer experience can be achieved through the magic of design.

### Education

Bachelors in journalism and mass communication from Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University, Delhi.

### About my role

I joined GO MO Group in 2016. I have had the opportunity to build and lead a team of designers and developers in producing digital products.

In my 8 years of leading design engagements, I’ve designed UI/UX strategies that operate in tandem with digital marketing for both B2B and B2C customers. I believe that a combination of user-centric design, effective stakeholder management, and digital marketing knowledge can bring about the best solutions for any digital product.

### Up close and personal

Curiosity and creative expression are the two mantras I live by. Because of this, I spend my free time indulging in my many hobbies and interests: reading fiction and history books, painting, writing, learning new languages, and making music, to name a few. Although I love meeting new people and traveling to new places, I feel equally joyous when I complete a painting or write a song alone in my room.
