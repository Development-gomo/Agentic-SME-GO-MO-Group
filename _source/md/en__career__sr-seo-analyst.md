### Scope of Work

**Research and Analysis:**

1. Keyword research as per client’s business
2. Provide recommendations for the client’s website
3. Use tools to conduct website diagnosis
4. Knowledge of web analytic tools like Adobe Analytics, Google Analytics 4, Search Console, Ahrefs, etc

**Communication:**

1. Coordinate with team Sweden on project performance, timelines and results
2. Discuss strategies and tasks with Project Managers in India

**Strategy & Execution:**

1. Understanding client’s online goals
2. Develop and execute strategies to achieve set goals and deliver impact
3. Spot new opportunities for account expansion & optimization

**Reporting:**

1. Preparation of performance reports & review with clients

### Key Qualification Criteria

- Minimum **1.5 years** of hands experience on SEO (compulsory)
- Ability to create impactful use cases for client and execute
- Outcome focused mindset to deliver value for client
- Expertise in solving problems for **Fortune B2B clients**
- Bachelor’s Degree in any field

### Preferred Attributes

- Experience in dealing with **international clients**
- Great **English** verbal and written communication skills
- Skilled in creating impactful **PowerPoint** presentations
- Has worked with **Adobe Analytics, GA4 and Custom CMS**
- Possesses a strong understanding of the **B2B Fortune** space
- Residing in **Pune** or willing to relocate to Pune
