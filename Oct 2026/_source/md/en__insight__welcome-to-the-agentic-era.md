# Welcome to the Agentic Era: Where your next customer might be an AI

_The buyer journey is being reshaped by AI agents, here’s why B2B enterprises need to get ahead of the change now_

![](https://www.gomogroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/case-banner-2.jpg.webp)

## **A change has begun. The agentic search era is here**

We’ve spent the last few months at GO MO Group researching one of the most profound shifts in digital strategy since the rise of Google: the emergence of AI agents as autonomous decision-makers, buyers, and intermediaries in the B2B landscape. The shift is happening right before our eyes, for those willing to look. OpenAI’s ChatGPT Agent (formerly Operator) is live and evolving.

Unlike traditional AI tools, these agents are able to take action. They browse, compare, and can make purchasing decisions autonomously. So let us officially **welcome you to the era of Business to Agents (B2A):** Where brands must serve not only human users, but also AI agents acting on their behalf.

## **What an agentic AI is, and why the industry will never be the same**

In January 2025, OpenAI launched “Operator,” now known as ChatGPT Agent. It allows users to issue complex commands like "Restock my groceries, book my flight, and find a contractor for next week." The Agent is able to execute on commands as well as search. It interacts with websites as if it were a human: clicking buttons, filling forms, navigating multiple browser tabs and even making purchases on a user’s behalf. It’s capable of:

It interacts with websites as if it were a human: clicking buttons, filling forms, navigating multiple browser tabs and even making purchases on a user’s behalf. It’s capable of:

- **Simulating human browsing** behavior across ecommerce and service platforms
- **Executing parallel tasks** (e.g., shopping and booking travel simultaneously)
- **Learning user preferences** (e.g., “no dairy” or “avoid certain brands”)
- **Bypassing UI entirely**, relying instead on structured data, schemas, and APIs when available

For brands that aren't ready, this shift will be invisible but costly. Agents will simply ignore content they can't understand, trust, or act upon.

## **The rise of “Business to Agents” (B2A): What it means for enterprises**

At GO MO Group, we define **B2A** as a strategic framework where businesses optimise their digital presence for **AI agents**, not just for human decision-makers.

Think of it this way:

- Traditional digital marketing was B2C or B2B.
- [GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)](https://www.gomogroup.com/en/insight/ai-in-marketing-the-end-of-website-traffic-as-we-know-it/) focused on **AI-curated search results**.
- Now, B2A focuses on **AI-executed actions** (like purchase, booking, or comparison).

In this new world, agents are becoming the new buyer personas. And just like we’ve designed websites for mobile users or international audiences, we now need to design them for autonomous agents.

![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%200%200'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## **Case studies: Who’s already leading in the agentic ecosystem?**

### **1\. Instacart:** **_User-friendly and agent-usable_**

Instacart didn’t wait for agents to “figure out” its platform. It built a structured, agent-ready infrastructure with:

- Public APIs for products, prices, and availability
- Schema-enhanced listings for machine comprehension
- Backend preference models for personalized recommendations

Now, ChatGPT Agents can shop Instacart like expert users but autonomously. Instacart isn’t just compatible with agents. It’s becoming a **preferred endpoint** for them.

### **2\. eBay:** **_Agentic discovery for complex buying behavior_**

eBay have optimised discovery for enthusiast buyers with long-tail preferences, by promoting discovery. By integrating with ChatGPT Agent and investing in their own in-house AI systems, eBay is reshaping how niche products are surfaced and recommended by AI agents to humans.

## **5 Strategic actions for enterprises ready to prepare for the agentic future**

1. **Audit your site’s machine readability**


Agents rely on structured content. Schema markup, product metadata, and clear taxonomies will be key.
2. **Develop open, modular APIs**


Design for AI as well as for humans. Agents can interact faster, more accurately, and with greater reliability through documented APIs.
3. **Define your Agent Personas**


What data would an agent need to represent your ideal customer? Use CRM + behavior analytics to shape agent-ready user journeys.
4. **Track Agent-Driven Interactions**


New analytics tools will emerge to track how agents interact with your site. Start preparing internal workflows to interpret this data.
5. **Stay Ahead of Interoperability Standards** Just like with AMP or schema.org, established agent interoperability standards are coming. Stay informed and participate early.
