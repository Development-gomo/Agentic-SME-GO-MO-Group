# Meet Safa

I’m Safa, managing director of GO MO Group in India. I’ve been stationed at our Pune office since 2013, and love to be able to work closely with our teams of specialists. I have always been an entrepreneur, and have previously worked in São Paulo, Brazil, and Gurgaon, India.

### Education

MSc in International Business and Management from the University of Manchester and a BSc in Business Administration with an emphasis in Management from California State University: Long Beach.

### About my role

I have a passion for international business and marketing. When I got the chance to combine these two at GO MO Group, I immediately jumped at the opportunity. I believe that a team with the right coordination and leadership can achieve things way above any individual’s capacity. My position between the Swedish and Indian offices makes me a key player in enabling our respective teams to perform at their best.

### Up close and personal

In my free time, I enjoy reading books on personal growth, and I am a true foodie – the only thing that can make good company better is good food. Especially if this combination happens to coincide with a win for Chelsea FC.
