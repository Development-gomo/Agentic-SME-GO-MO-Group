### Agenda

Built on our own client research and interviews with companies such as Google, Semrush, Profound, and Adobe, this session introduces the new field of GEO (or AEO, AIO) and what it takes to make your brand visible and recommended by AI and agents. We combine education and strategic insight with practical, hands-on takeaways, supported by real examples from B2B companies.

- 07:30

**Doors open**


Coffee, breakfast, and networking

- 08:00

**Changes we are seeing in the B2B landscape right now**


The big technology shifts behind AI search, agents, and the new compressed discovery journey, and how this is impacting traffic, brand visibility, and buying behaviour

- 08:20

**From SEO to GEO and what to measure now**


What is actually different, what still matters, where the big shift is happening, and which targets and KPIs matter in this new domain compared with traditional search metrics

- 08:40

- 10:00

**Networking and optional mini-audit walkthroughs**


Continues until 11:00 for those who want to stay

Who is this event for?

The session is designed primarily for marketing and communications leaders at large companies — CMOs, Digital Directors, Heads of Growth, and similar roles. That said, we also welcome senior marketers from mid-sized companies and portfolio operations leads at investment groups. If you’re responsible for how your brand shows up to customers, this is relevant.

What can I practically take with me?

You’ll leave with a clear understanding of how AI platforms currently evaluate and recommend brands, and a concrete framework for what to prioritise. Every attendee also receives a personalized AI Brand Visibility Snapshot within two weeks – a custom analysis of how AI sees your brand across your most important commercial queries.

Can I bring a colleague?

Absolutely – we encourage it. Having multiple perspectives in the room makes the conversation richer. Each person needs to submit their own seat request so we can ensure a relevant audience

Is the event free of charge?

Yes, there’s no cost to attend. Since we’re keeping the session small and focused, we review all seat requests to make sure the room is composed of marketing and communications professionals who’ll get the most out of it.
