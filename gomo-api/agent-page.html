Agentic Web

GO MO Group builds a machine-facing 'agentic web' layer on a company's existing domain so AI assistants and agentic browsers can read, cite and act on its business information. The layer has six components: an /agent page, llms.txt, schema.org structured data, a /.well-known API catalogue, a read-only public REST API and an MCP server. A standard build runs about three weeks (audit, build, deploy, verify) and is followed by optional monthly sync; the existing human website is not rebuilt.

Den agentiska webben är det maskininriktade lagret av din webbplats – ditt företag som strukturerad data som AI-assistenter och agentiska webbläsare kan läsa, citera och agera utifrån. GO MO bygger lagret på er egen domän, bredvid den befintliga webbplatsen.

  • Six components: agent page (/agent), llms.txt, schema.org structured data, API catalogue (/.well-known), public read-only REST API (/api/v1) and MCP server (/mcp)
  • Four-step delivery in about three weeks: audit, build, deploy on the client's own domain (robots.txt and ai.txt permissions set), verify and keep in sync monthly
  • Measured three ways: Lighthouse/PageSpeed Insights 'Agentic Browsing' category (score out of 3), independent audits by Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity, and a live MCP test where an agent submits an enquiry with user consent
  • First Swedish B2B build: Agentic Browsing 1/3 to 3/3, 5/5 AI platforms rated it agent-friendly, 2/2 agent-submitted leads delivered via MCP, 8+/10 average LLM answer rating
  • Google's share of search fell to 75.5% (Statcounter, 2020 vs 2026)
  • 53% of web traffic is no longer human (Imperva Bad Bot Report, 2026)
  • Almost no competitor in industrial B2B has a machine-readable layer today (GO MO Group, market review)
  • No website rebuild required: the layer is added alongside the existing site on the same domain

FAQ

What is the agentic web?

The agentic web is the machine-facing layer of a website. It presents the same business information as structured, readable data so AI assistants and agentic browsers can retrieve it, cite it and act on it without parsing a page built for human eyes.

Source: https://www.gomogroup.com/en/services/agentic-web/

How is this different from SEO or GEO?

SEO and GEO influence whether you are found and recommended. The agentic web decides what an agent can actually read and do once it arrives. They are complementary: visibility brings the agent, the agentic layer serves it.

Source: https://www.gomogroup.com/en/services/agentic-web/

Do we need to rebuild our website?

No. The agentic layer is added alongside your existing site. Your human website stays exactly as it is; we build a parallel machine-readable layer on the same domain.

Source: https://www.gomogroup.com/en/services/agentic-web/

How long does an agentic web build take?

A standard build runs about three weeks from audit to verified deployment. Larger sites with multiple markets or product catalogues take longer, mostly in data mapping.

Source: https://www.gomogroup.com/en/services/agentic-web/

Why does it need monthly upkeep?

Your website keeps changing, new crawlers and browser agents keep arriving, and the specifications themselves are still moving. Without upkeep the layer drifts out of sync and agents answer from stale data.

Source: https://www.gomogroup.com/en/services/agentic-web/

How do you measure whether it worked?

Four measures: the Lighthouse agentic browsing category, how well leading models describe your business when asked, agent and crawler traffic in your logs, and citation appearances in AI answers.

Source: https://www.gomogroup.com/en/services/agentic-web/

What’s the difference between YouTube ads and a YouTube marketing strategy?

YouTube ads generate views while your budget is active — when it stops, visibility stops. A YouTube marketing strategy builds organic discovery through search-optimised content, channel architecture, and playlist structure. The goal is content that compounds in value over time — reaching buyers through YouTube Search, Suggested Videos, and AI platforms without ongoing media spend per view. Both have a role, but most B2B companies are 100% dependent on paid. We change that ratio.

Source: https://www.gomogroup.com/en/services/b2b-youtube-marketing/

Do we need to create a lot of new videos to see results?

Not necessarily — and often not at all in Month 1. The highest-impact improvements almost always come from optimising what already exists: rewriting metadata, adding chapter structure, adjusting visibility settings, reorganising channel architecture, and building playlists. Most companies have a video library that significantly underperforms its potential. We unlock that first, then build a content strategy around proven demand.

Source: https://www.gomogroup.com/en/services/b2b-youtube-marketing/

Is YouTube actually relevant for B2B companies in industrial or technical sectors?

Especially so. Technical buyers actively use YouTube to understand how products work, compare specifications, and validate decisions before involving procurement. Categories like industrial equipment, construction machinery, logistics software, enterprise technology, and professional services all have genuine search demand on YouTube. The opportunity is large precisely because most B2B companies in these sectors have not yet invested in organic YouTube strategy — which means first movers will compound their advantage.

Source: https://www.gomogroup.com/en/services/b2b-youtube-marketing/

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) focuses on ranking in traditional search engines like Google — getting your page to appear at the top of a results page. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) focuses on getting your brand cited and recommended within AI-generated answers from tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. The key difference: in SEO you earn a position in a list. In GEO you earn a mention within a conversational answer. The signals that drive each are related but distinct — GEO places greater weight on entity authority, citation across trusted sources, and content that answers questions comprehensively.

Source: https://www.gomogroup.com/en/services/generative-engine-optimization-geo/

What is Generative Engine Optimization?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of structuring your brand’s digital presence so that AI language models and search engines — like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google’s AI Overviews — are more likely to reference, cite, and recommend your brand when users ask relevant questions. It involves optimizing content for how LLMs crawl and process information, building entity authority across the web, and ensuring your brand is clearly associated with the topics and problems your customers are asking AI about.

Source: https://www.gomogroup.com/en/services/generative-engine-optimization-geo/

Is GEO replacing SEO?

Not replacing — but fundamentally reshaping. Traditional SEO remains important, especially for transactional queries and local search. But the share of research and discovery happening through AI search is growing rapidly, particularly in B2B contexts where buyers use tools like ChatGPT to evaluate vendors before ever visiting a website. The smartest approach is to treat GEO and SEO as complementary disciplines. Many of the foundations — strong content, authoritative backlinks, technical health — benefit both. GEO adds a new layer: entity building, AI citation optimization, and prompt-level visibility tracking.

Source: https://www.gomogroup.com/en/services/generative-engine-optimization-geo/
