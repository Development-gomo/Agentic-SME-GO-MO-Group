How it works

## The ad appears when  it fits the conversation

ChatGPT considers the context and intent of the conversation when it selects an ad.

+

“We need a flexible office for 40 people with good public transport.”

ChatGPT checks relevance

+

It weighs the conversation, the ad’s title and description, the landing page and the advertiser’s context hints.

Step 3

A relevant ad may appear

+

Below the response, labelled as sponsored and kept separate from the answer.

Step 4

The user visits your site

+

The click lands on a relevant page. Pixel, conversion events and UTMs measure what happens next.

Built in by OpenAI

## Clear rules for  ads in ChatGPT

✓

##### Context, not keywords

Context hints describe when an offer is useful. They work differently from exact-match keywords.

✓

##### Answers stay independent

Ads do not influence ChatGPT’s answers.

✓

##### Conversations stay private

Advertisers never get access to users’ conversations.

✓

##### Results you can see

Impressions, clicks, spend and conversions are reported in Ads Manager.

## Three lessons we bring  to your pilot

From the first reporting week of our live pilot, 15–21 September 2026.

01 · Cost per click

##### Traffic costs less than planned

Clicks came in at about half the planned cost, so the same budget buys more learning. Planned 30–50 SEK; actual ≈16 SEK (4,589 SEK spend ÷ 279 clicks).

02 · Campaign type

##### Broad needs perform best

The generic, need-based campaign delivered the most clicks (129) at the lowest cost, around 14–16 SEK per click.

03 · Click-through by city

##### Context hints steer relevance

Click rates differ clearly by city: Malmö 2.02%, Stockholm ≈1.2%, Göteborg ≈1.2%. That tells us where to shift budget and creative next.

Common questions

## ChatGPT Ads, answered

What are ChatGPT Ads?

Sponsored cards that appear below ChatGPT’s answer when they fit the conversation. They are labelled as sponsored and kept separate from the answer.

Who sees the ads?

Eligible adult users on the Free and Go plans.

Can Swedish companies advertise today?

Yes. Ads Manager self-service is open to Swedish-billed entities, so there is no waiting list.

Do ads influence ChatGPT’s answers?

No. Ads do not influence the answers, and advertisers never get access to users’ conversations.

How is this different from keyword ads?

You write context hints that describe when your offer is useful. ChatGPT weighs the conversation, the ad’s title and description, the landing page and those hints. They work differently from exact-match keywords.

What does a click cost?

On our Swedish runs we have paid about 16–18 SEK per click, roughly half OpenAI’s guided 30–50 SEK.

How is it billed?

Per valid click, in both pilot phases.

How do you measure results?

OpenAI Pixel and conversion events, UTM tagging on every ad, GA4 and lead matching in your CRM. You get a report every week.

What do we need to do?

Create the ad account, add billing and invite GO MO. We handle tracking, campaigns, creative and launch.

How does this relate to GEO?

Ads put you below the answer from day one. GEO works on getting you named in the answer over time, and ad insights show which topics to own.
