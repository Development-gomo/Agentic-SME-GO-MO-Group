# Take the Stage Strategically

Case study \| Digital strategy \| SEO & Online reputation management

### About the client

A top-ranked global leader in advanced transport and infrastructure solutions, this European company generated sales of 50 Billion USD in 2024. With a strong history, they operate in over 190 countries, serving industries such as automotive, energy, and construction. Recognized for its emphasis on sustainability, electrification, and innovation, the organization leverages digital transformation as a driving force for growth and efficiency.

Opportunity

### Becoming  ‘Data-driven’ in PR & Event Management to steer investments without guesswork.

### Strategy

Challenge

Traditional PR methodologies lacked precision in identifying relevant events. Significant roadblocks include:

- Missed opportunities
- Lack of digital leverage
- Inefficient resource allocation

This is where digital offers a new way to pursue data-driven PR with metrics such as:

- Search Potential for relevant topics
- Web Presence
- Social Proof and Background

![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201920%20960'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The result?

### Optimized PR investments, uncovering high-impact opportunities for enhanced brand visibility

We’ve seen this pattern time and again: large B2B companies under-leverage data, leaving PR as the least-funded and least-prioritized function.

Even with just a pilot project, we’ve already seen how data-driven insights can deliver tangible results.

Most PR-related decisions are taken without digital data involvement –  In such a space how do we stay proactive? By insisting on the value of data and demonstrating that a well-executed PR strategy isn’t a luxury—it’s a growth driver.
