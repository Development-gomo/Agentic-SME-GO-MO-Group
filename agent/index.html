Frequently Asked Questions about GO MO Group

What is GO MO Group?

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO focuses on optimizing content with keywords to rank well on traditional search engines. GEO complements this by addressing AI-driven algorithms, adding layers of context and relevance that go beyond keywords to enhance content interaction with AI technologies such as Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity.

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is an advanced strategy that complements traditional SEO by optimizing content for the nuances of AI search engines. It ensures content is not only visible but also highly relevant and engaging according to AI processing and interpretation standards. GEO includes technical website optimisation, content hub development, FAQ strategy, backlink building, brand mention cultivation, schema markup, review platform presence, and multimedia content creation.

Is GEO replacing SEO?

No, GEO does not replace SEO but rather builds upon it. It introduces sophisticated AI-focused strategies to traditional SEO methods, aiming to further enhance content visibility and engagement in the evolving landscape of AI-driven search environments. SEO gets a brand found; GEO gets a brand recommended.

What is Pull Marketing?

Pull Marketing is marketing to individuals and organisations with an expressed demand for one's services or products. By targeting an interested audience, companies capitalise on existing demand and ensure they are part of each potential client's buying journey. 94% of all B2B buying processes begin online, and 87% of research occurs via Google or search engines. GO MO Group's Pull Marketing methodology integrates GEO, SEO, SEA, content marketing, and ASBX outbound into one coherent strategy.

What is ASBX?

ASBX (Account and Segment-Based Experience) is GO MO Group's proprietary B2B outbound lead generation service. It delivers contacts ready to speak to a client's sales team through a four-step process: identify target company types, locate prospects within valuable segments, create segment-specific messaging, and execute through email, digital ads, and landing pages. ASBX can generate up to 1,000 key prospects annually for B2B SaaS/Tech clients.

What is Enterprise SEO and how does it differ from traditional SEO?

Enterprise SEO focuses on large-scale strategies tailored for complex B2B organisations, optimising high-volume websites, multi-regional and multi-language content, and aligning with sales and marketing objectives. Unlike traditional SEO which addresses smaller websites, enterprise SEO involves cross-departmental coordination, advanced technical implementations, and attribution modelling at scale.
