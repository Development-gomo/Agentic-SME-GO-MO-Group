## Our Cookie Policy

GO MO Group AB uses cookies. According to the Electronic Communications Act that came into force on July 25, 2003, all visitors to websites that use cookies must be informed about this. A cookie is a short text that is sent from a website to your browser and then stored in your system. GO MO Group uses cookies to personalize the information on our website so that it matches the visitor’s preferences as far as possible. Cookies also customize the information displayed on the site the next time you visit it. If you have registered on the site, certain information, such as your username, is stored via a cookie.

## Options

If you do not wish to accept cookies from the GO MO Group website, you may choose to leave the website or configure your browser to either notify you when you receive a cookie (so that you can choose whether or not to accept it) or block all or some types of cookies. The browser can also delete previously saved cookies. Information about cookie settings can be found in the browser’s help function. Even if you have blocked the use of cookies in your browser, you can visit most parts of GO MO Group’s websites.

We use cookies to be sure to give you the best experience of our websites and web applications.
