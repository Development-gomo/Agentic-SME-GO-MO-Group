## ASBX vs Outreach Tools

There are many great tools for salespeople – many of which we use ourselves. Salespeople can now find prospects, sort these into campaigns, create automated outreach sequences, A/B test email copy, and engage thousands of prospects automatically.

Only, that isn’t automatic; optimizing that kind of sales process takes a lot of work. We propose to do all of that for you, so your sales organization can _actually_ just sit back and get warm leads to their inbox.

### Account-Based   Outreach

Immediately get valuable contact information to decision-makers in your target segment. We will use email marketing automation to communicate your offer, reaching out in a personalized way. This outreach can be combined with activities from your sales organization to increase the odds for B2B sales.

### Segment-Based   Value Proposition

The backbone of effective marketing is _relevance_ – matching your audience with offers they would be interested in. We plan the communication of your products and services according to the needs of each specific segment.

### Marketing   Materials Creation

Our expert copywriters will create email copy to effectively reach your potential buyers. We also design and develop landing pages for the email and Linkedin campaigns.

### All   Together Now

By employing a multichannel approach, exposing your potential buyers to your brand and offering on Linkedin while the email campaigns go out the personal, account-level contact will not be completely cold, increasing the chances for conversion.
