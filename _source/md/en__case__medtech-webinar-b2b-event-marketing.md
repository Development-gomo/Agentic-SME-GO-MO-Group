The brief

## Not a headline number. A room full of budget holders.

A medical-technology manufacturer was launching a new platform into a fast-expanding market. The webinar had to put heads of department, programme directors and education leads in front of the product on one live date, in two countries.

Challenge

### The hard part

One live date, no rolling deadline, and a senior clinical audience that books weeks ahead and cancels easily. A slow start in week one would have meant no way to make the date up later. Awareness and urgency had to build in the same four weeks.

Success criteria

### The measure

Registrations were the KPI, but attendance and audience quality were the real test. A full list of the wrong people would have been a failure, even at target.

The target

The strategy

## Surround the market. Convert on one page.

Each channel was given one job in the funnel: reach, depth or precision. All of them sent people to a single destination.

Channel 01 · Reach & familiarity

~40%of all registrations

### Paid social

Build familiarity at scale

The widest, cheapest reach in the mix. Interest and job-title targeting warmed the professional community across both markets, so the invitation landed with people who already recognised the story. Every viewer became a retargetable engager for the closing fortnight.

~60%of all qualified reach

Lowestcost per thousand reached

Channel 02 · Depth & commitment

~25%of all registrations

### Email & owned

Convert the people who already know you

A passive placement in every outbound sales signature did most of the work, supported by CRM sequences and an on-site prompt. This audience already had a relationship with the brand, and it turned up at roughly twice the rate of cold paid reach.

~51%show-up rate, the best of any channel

~4owned touchpoints in play

Channel 03 · Precision & credibility

~35%of all registrations

### Network & outreach

Reach named decision-makers directly

A matched audience of ~2,100 named leaders on the professional network, plus organic amplification from a known voice in the field and personalised outreach to priority accounts. Precision rather than volume.

~2,100named leaders matched

~69%show-up rate on organic reach

After the event

### The list outlived the event.

Every registrant became a first-party, retargetable audience: a warm pool the client can reach again for the next launch without paying to prospect it a second time.

What transfers

## Three habits from this launch, and where we take them next.

01

### One job per channel

Paid social delivered **~40%** of registrations; owned email converted at **~51%**. Asking one channel to do both is how event campaigns underfill.

02

### Track every source to one page

Every channel pointed to the exact same registration page, with nine tracked sources feeding it. That meant **100%** of registrations carried a known source, so budget decisions were never guesswork.

03

### Front-load the list, back-load the push

Across the four-week campaign, **~62%** of registrations arrived in the closing two weeks. The early weeks build the pool; the closing sequence is what converts it.
