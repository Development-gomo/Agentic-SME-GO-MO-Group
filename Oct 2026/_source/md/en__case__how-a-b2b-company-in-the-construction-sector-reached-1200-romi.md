# How a B2B company in the construction sector achieved 1200% ROMI, despite a historically weak year for the industry

Case study \| SME

2024 and 2025 were among the toughest years the construction sector has faced in a long time, making marketing in the construction industry more challenging than ever. More than 2,000 construction companies have gone bankrupt since 2024\*. This, combined with reduced investments, delayed projects, and severely constrained budgets, has led many players to experience sharp declines in both order intake and profitability\*, severely impacting sales and marketing efforts in the construction industry.

Despite this, a leading B2B e-commerce player in the construction industry has managed to maintain stable and predictable sales through its website during this period – demonstrating how effective marketing a construction business can be even in a downturn. Together with GO MO Group, a marketing agency with specialisation in construction, the company built a digital strategy that withstood market volatility and continues to generate business regardless of economic conditions.

## A marketing strategy driving sales in a construction industry facing headwinds

With a declining market, the client’s focus was clear:

To make it as easy as possible for buyers to convert digitally, using best practices from digital marketing for construction companies.

This resulted in:

- Improved conversion paths for quote requests, supporting scalable lead generation
- Direct purchases of bundled solutions via on-site payment options
- A more sales-driven and frictionless e-commerce experience

At a time when many in the industry are struggling with volumes, this client has instead operated a continuously generating digital sales channel that creates stability and predictability – proving the value of marketing a construction business with the right agency partner.

## The keys behind growth despite tough market conditions in the construction industry

### Scalable paid marketing

In 2025, market conditions became significantly tougher. Demand declined while competitors ramped up their digital investments, creating a more competitive and pressured environment. Instead of becoming passive in a shifting market, the company chose to double down on the channels it knew worked and doubled its investments in Google and Bing Ads – core channels within digital marketing for construction companies. Through this persistent strategy, the company strengthened its visibility, continued to gain market share, and drove sales despite both lower demand and increased competition.

### SEO & AI search (GEO)

An optimised search experience drove more qualified traffic and made it significantly easier for users to quickly find the right solution. By improving category pages, creating a more logical product structure, and ensuring that relevant information was easily accessible, the buying experience became both clearer and more efficient – strengthening long-term lead generation.

### Email campaigns targeting RAM contract customers

Segmented campaigns, especially those aimed at RAM contract customers, strengthened base sales while increasing engagement over time. By tailoring content and offers to customer needs, email became a stable and recurring source of revenue.

## The result: 1200% ROMI in a declining industry

Digital channels generated a total of 12x more than the resources invested. This means:

**Every krona invested returned more than 12 kronor, during a year when large parts of the industry experienced a significant decline in sales, reinforcing the impact of professional digital marketing for companies in the construction sector.**

## In summary

When the construction sector faced its weakest year in a long time, the client demonstrated that the right digital strategy can function as a stable sales platform, independent of market conditions. By combining performance marketing, SEO, AI search, and optimised conversion flows, the company created predictable, scalable, and profitable digital sales – and achieved a ROMI of over 1200%, clearly illustrating the power of a specialised construction marketing agency and advanced marketing for companies in the construction industry.
