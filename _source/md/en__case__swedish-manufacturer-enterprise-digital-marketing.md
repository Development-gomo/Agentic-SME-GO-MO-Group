Enterprise Digital Marketing for a Swedish Manufacturer

# How we leveraged search to   increase share of digital revenue   from 2% to 7%

Case study \| SEO & Web content \| Paid Ads

### About the client

A global leader in industrial solutions with reported record revenues of EUR 10+ billion in 2023, specializing in advanced technologies. With a rich history and a presence in over 180 countries, they serve industries such as manufacturing, energy, and construction by providing transformative technologies that power industries worldwide. Renowned for their commitment to innovation and efficiency, a strong internal leader and advocate for digital marketing has identified that digital growth can be a key driver for efficient and sustainable growth.

Opportunity

### Increase the digital revenue for one of their business areas for a Swedish Manufacturing Giant.

### Strategy

Problem

Operating in a diverse and competitive market like India, the client faced significant challenges in driving digital revenue. Traditional marketing frameworks, slow decision-making processes, and limited website update capabilities created bottlenecks.

These constraints, combined with limited SEM budgets, hindered efforts to implement effective digital strategies, starting from a low baseline of 2% revenue contribution.

![SEO S Curve and SEM to bridge the gap](https://www.gomogroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Charts-960x430.png.webp)

Strategic Approach

To overcome these limitations and achieve immediate impact while building for long-term growth, we employed a dual-pronged strategy.

This included:

- **Google Ads Optimization**


Driving immediate visibility and conversions through targeted paid campaigns.
- **Enterprise SEO Enhancements**


Building a strong foundation for sustainable growth with advanced strategies like dynamic content creation and smart backlink optimization.

The result?

### Increase in digital revenue from 2% to 7% within 36 months.

**318%**

Increase in digital leads, including a 43% drop in cost per lead year over year and significant traffic increase.

**12%**

share of voice on organic sessions, showing a remarkable improvement from 4.72% to 11.46%.

**20%**

increase in organic sessions, showcasing substantial growth in organic visibility for target keywords.
