# From Uddevalla to Global Markets

_The case was first published in 2024 — and has since been continuously updated, with the work still ongoing as of March 2026. The journey has evolved and deepened over time. Below, we share insights from the starting point to where things stand today — and what has actually happened along the way._

**How FKAB Marine Design Began Its AI-Driven Growth Journey**

![](https://www.gomogroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/fkab-ship-image1-495x400-1.webp)

How can a ship design company from Uddevalla, Sweden expand globally while making its sales and marketing efforts more efficient?

For FKAB Marine Design, the answer was a combination of **strategy, data, and AI**. Together with GO MO Group, the company began an AI-driven transformation aimed at building a more targeted marketing strategy and strengthening its international presence.

## About the Company

FKAB Marine Design is a Swedish engineering and design company specializing in ship design for international markets. With deep roots in western Sweden and clients around the world, the company has long delivered innovative vessel design solutions to the maritime industry.

As the market evolves and competition increases, FKAB recognized the need to modernize its sales and marketing processes to support continued growth.

> “We want to grow and go even further, and to do that we need to keep up.”
>
> – Peter Hermansson, CEO, FKAB Marine Design

## The Challenge

FKAB faced a critical question: how can the company continue expanding globally while making its marketing efforts more efficient and data-driven?

To achieve this, the organization needed to:

- identify new international markets
- work more systematically with data and insights
- streamline marketing and sales processes
- leverage emerging technologies to stay competitive

Addressing these challenges required both **strategic analysis and new ways of working.**

## The Solution: A Strategic AI Feasibility Study

The collaboration with GO MO Group began with a comprehensive feasibility study focusing on FKAB’s market opportunities and digital demand.

The goal was to establish a clear foundation for how FKAB could integrate AI and data into its growth strategy.

The work focused on three key areas:

**Market Analysis** Identifying priority markets and assessing global demand.

**Data-Driven Marketing Strategy** Developing targeted marketing activities based on insights and analysis.

**AI-Powered Optimization** Using generative AI to support content creation, campaign optimization, and analytical processes.

The result was a clear roadmap for how FKAB can strengthen its marketing capabilities through data and AI.

## DigiShift – The Starting Point for FKAB’s AI Journey

FKAB took its first concrete steps into AI through the **DigiShift program by the West Sweden Chamber of Commerce**, where GO MO Group participates as a partner.

The program provides companies with:

- inspiration and education around AI
- access to experts
- a network of companies undergoing similar transformations

The initiative began in the summer of 2024 with the goal of building a strong foundation for targeted campaigns in selected international markets.

Throughout the process, GO MO Group acted as a strategic partner, helping FKAB translate insights into practical actions.

## Results

The collaboration has already led to several tangible improvements within the organization.

**More Precise Marketing Activities** Data-driven insights now allow FKAB to target its marketing efforts toward the most relevant markets.

**More Efficient Internal Processes** AI has helped automate parts of reporting and analytical work.

**Greater Focus on Strategic Initiatives** The management team can now spend more time on long-term decision-making and business development.

> “By automating parts of our reporting and analytical processes, we’ve been able to free up time for strategic initiatives and long-term decisions.”
>
> – Peter Hermansson

## A Shift in How Businesses Operate

For FKAB, adopting AI is not just about implementing new technology. It represents a shift in how the organization approaches decision-making, analysis, and market strategy.

> “GenAI is no longer just a buzzword. It’s a tool that is already reshaping businesses and creating new opportunities.”
>
> – Gabriel Ghavami, CEO, GO MO Group

Companies that integrate data and AI into their decision-making processes are able to move faster, work more efficiently, and identify new opportunities earlier.

## Key Takeaways from the Project

The collaboration with FKAB highlights three important insights:

**AI delivers the greatest value when tied to business goals** Technology alone does not create value without a clear strategic context.

**Data-driven decision-making creates competitive advantage** Organizations that systematically use data move faster and make better decisions.

**AI requires new ways of working** Successful implementation involves both new tools and new mindsets.
