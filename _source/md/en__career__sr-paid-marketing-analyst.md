# Sr. Paid Marketing Analyst

[Apply for this job](https://www.gomogroup.com/en/career-form/?job=sr-paid-marketing-analyst)

## Key qualification criteria

**Qualifications:**

- Minimum 2-3 years of experience in paid ad marketing across digital channels: Meta, LinkedIn and Google Ads
- With at least 2 years of hands-on experience managing and optimizing Google Ads
- Strong domain expertise in paid media, including strategy, execution, and analytics
- Skilled in leveraging AI tools (e.g., Claude and ChatGPT,) to enhance campaign efficiency and decision-making
- Strong analytical skills with advanced proficiency in Excel or Google Sheets for data analysis and performance tracking
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English
- Bachelor’s degree in any field; a Master’s degree is a plus

**Experience:**

- Hands-on experience with:
  - Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Bing/Microsoft Ads
  - Google Tag Manager (GTM), GA4, Looker Studio
- Strong understanding of performance marketing principles, bidding strategies, and attribution models
- Familiarity with SEO, email marketing, and other digital marketing channels.
- Certifications in tools such as Google Ads, GA4, or Meta Ads are preferred
- Proven track record in driving successful Performance Campaigns utilizing AI tools like ChatGPT
- Effective communication and reporting skills
- Good experience in managing medium to large-sized budgets
- Should have a data driven approach to work

## Scope of work

**Research and Analysis:**

- Stay ahead of platform updates and trends; integrate innovations into strategy
- Drive keyword strategy and restructure accounts to unlock scalable growth
- Lead cross-channel campaign optimization using platforms like Google Ads, Bing Ads, and GA4 to improve ROI
- Analyze and interpret conversion and attribution data to derive actionable strategic insights
- Ability to create strategies and budget forecasts

**Strategy & Execution:**

- Lead paid media initiatives to drive conversions and revenue using omnichannel strategies, capitalizing on AI for optimization.
- Optimize campaigns on platforms like Google Ads, Bing Ads, and LinkedIn Ads
- Take ownership of campaigns end-to-end to ensure KPIs and Goals are met
- Continuously refine team processes and automate repetitive tasks to drive efficiency and scalability

**Communication:**

- Collaborate with Client Success Managers to design growth-focused strategies that leverage AI capabilities
- Partner with internal teams to address performance challenges
- Timely Deliver projects on time, meeting weekly and monthly deadlines

**Reporting:**

- Create performance reports using Google Slides/other dashboards, and review them with clients
- Present key strategies and insights to Client Success Managers and clients
- Consider key performance metrics such as CPC, CTR, CPA, & ROAS for creating and conveying detailed inferences in monthly, quarterly & yearly reports

## Preferred Attributes

- Certifications in digital marketing tools like GA4, Google Ads, Meta Ads, etc
- Must be residing in/ willing to relocate to Pune
- Experience with delivery management or people management
- Understanding of SEO, email marketing, and other digital marketing channels
- Experience with GA4, Google Tag Manager (GTM), Google Ads, Looker Studio and Bing Ads
- Familiarity with automated or AI-driven optimization tools for search ads and other channels
- Experience in dealing directly with B2B clients
