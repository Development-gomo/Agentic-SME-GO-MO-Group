# Meet Ida

As a Project and Operations Coordinator, I am dedicated to optimizing our project execution and operational efficiency. My key responsibilities include:

**Project Management:** I oversee timelines and coordinate across teams to ensure that all projects meet their deadlines and align with client expectations. My role is crucial in managing the flow of information and resources to guarantee seamless project progression.

**Operational Support:** I am committed to enhancing operational processes to boost efficiency across the board. This includes managing office logistics, assisting with budget and financial tracking, and serving as a ”mood manager” at our Gothenburg office to ensure that all staff are content and well-equipped for their daily tasks.

**Team Collaboration:** I facilitate robust communication channels, coordinate travel and events for the team, support CEO logistics, and address project hurdles. My focus is on fostering a cohesive and productive environment that enhances team performance and project outcomes.

Since joining GO MO Group at its inception in 2012, I have played a pivotal role in the evolution of the company. My journey has allowed me to interact closely with every department, witnessing and contributing to the growth of our service offerings. This extensive experience, combined with my visits to our Pune office in India, has enriched my understanding and approach, enabling me to drive initiatives that support both our team’s welfare and our clients’ success.

### Education

Undergrad in business and administration and multiple certificates within Google tools like Analytics and Ads.

### About my role

Today, as a Project and Operations Coordinator, I continue to build on the foundational experiences I gained as one of the “first five” employees at our fast-growing company. This unique position has provided me with invaluable flexibility, allowing me to explore various roles that enhance customer value and expand our service offerings.

In my current role, I am responsible for overseeing project timelines and ensuring seamless coordination across teams, which is vital for meeting client expectations and deadlines. My efforts to streamline processes contribute significantly to our operational efficiency, managing everything from office logistics to budget tracking. I also take pride in maintaining a positive atmosphere at our Gothenburg office, ensuring that all staff are content and well-equipped for their tasks.

Additionally, my role involves fostering team collaboration. I facilitate communication, coordinate events and travels, support our CEO’s logistics, and address any project hurdles. These responsibilities are critical in maintaining a cohesive and productive team environment.

### Up close and personal

The phrase ”being married to your job” takes on a literal and figurative meaning for me. Not only am I married to GO MO’s founder and CEO, but I also share a deep and enduring commitment to the company. I am truly passionate about my role at GO MO and thrive on the challenges and growth opportunities it presents. Consequently, discussions about work often find their way to our dinner table, making GO MO not just my workplace but a significant part of our family’s lifestyle.

Outside of work, I cherish spending time with our son, and together as a family, we love to travel—embracing both long and short journeys whenever we can. While I love to travel and discover new places, I equally enjoy a sweaty workout class, dinners with good friends, or a quiet night at home with a great book or movie.
